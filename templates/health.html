How to actually verify it

  • Look it up on Google Maps -- a real clinic has reviews, photos and a listed address that matches.
  • Call the number yourself using one you found independently, not one texted or emailed to you.
  • Check it's registered -- in India, ask for its Clinical Establishment / NABH registration number.
  • Never pay a "booking fee" or share OTPs to confirm an appointment -- real clinics don't ask for that.
  • When in doubt, use your campus health center instead.
