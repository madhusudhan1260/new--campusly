Health resources library

General, educational information -- not medical advice. For anything urgent or ongoing, see a doctor at {{ health_center.name }} or your own physician.

🩹 First aid
  • Clean a minor cut with running water before covering it.
  • For a sprain: rest, ice, compression, elevation.
  • Call for help immediately for chest pain, severe bleeding, or trouble breathing.
🥗 Nutrition
  • Regular meals help more than skipping and overcompensating later.
  • Keep water nearby -- thirst is often mistaken for hunger or fatigue.
  • A campus dietitian or doctor can help with specific dietary needs.
🧠 Mental wellness
  • Naming a feeling ("I'm stressed about X") reduces its intensity for many people.
  • Short breaks between study blocks help focus more than pushing through.
  • The counselor request above is confidential -- use it.
🚨 Emergency procedures
  • Use the SOS button above for a campus emergency -- it alerts the health team directly.
  • Know the location of {{ health_center.name }}: {{ health_center.location }}.
  • For life-threatening emergencies, also call local emergency services.
♀ Women's health
  • The campus health center can advise on reproductive and general women's health.
  • Bring a list of symptoms and their timing to any appointment -- it speeds up diagnosis.
🏃 Fitness
  • Even short daily walks measurably improve mood and sleep.
  • Warm up before intense exercise to reduce injury risk.
😴 Sleep
  • A consistent sleep/wake time matters more than total hours alone.
  • Screens before bed delay sleep onset for most people -- try a wind-down buffer.
🧘 Stress management
  • The breathing exercise above is a quick, evidence-based way to calm your nervous system.
  • Chronic stress is worth discussing with a counselor, not just managing alone.

How to actually verify it

  • Look it up on Google Maps -- a real clinic has reviews, photos and a listed address that matches.
  • Call the number yourself using one you found independently, not one texted or emailed to you.
  • Check it's registered -- in India, ask for its Clinical Establishment / NABH registration number.
  • Never pay a "booking fee" or share OTPs to confirm an appointment -- real clinics don't ask for that.
  • When in doubt, use your campus health center instead.
